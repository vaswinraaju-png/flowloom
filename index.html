FAQ

Questions

What is Flowloom?

A tool that reads code you paste in and turns it into an editable flowchart, right in your browser, using a rule-based parser Flowloom's team built. It's not an AI model; it's straightforward pattern matching on your code's structure.

Do I need to sign up?

No. Open the app and start pasting code. There is no account, no card, and no usage limit on the free tier.

Is my code sent anywhere?

No. Parsing happens entirely in your browser with JavaScript. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

What does the $9/year plan add?

It's entirely optional. It adds export to any format, including .drawio, SVG, and PDF, plus cloud save so your diagrams follow you across devices. It's currently on a waitlist.

What kind of code does it understand best?

Sequential guard-clause functions, a series of if (condition) return X; checks, which parse cleanly into branching flowcharts. More complex, deeply nested logic will parse into a best-effort chain you can then rearrange by hand.

Your code is never sent anywhere. Everything runs in your browser.
